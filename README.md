## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Mandate Order**. This trainer provides optional assistance with Mandate Order's city-management and warfare systems. It can simplify resource logistics, accelerate construction and production, support population and farming management, strengthen recruitment, and improve survivability during large-scale battles and fortress defense.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for players who want to experiment with city layouts, test production and logistics systems, develop different governing strategies, prepare large armies, or reduce repetitive management in single-player campaigns.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Mandate Order is controlled through a strategic city and campaign view rather than direct character movement. Use the trainer after loading a settlement or battle and keep the camera focused on the active stronghold or army. Movement-related assistance is intended for army positioning and campaign operations, not a third-person player character.

### Tools & Inventory

The game relies on managed supplies rather than a conventional character inventory. Use resource options for materials such as food, wood, weapons, and other city supplies. Activate resource assistance after the settlement has loaded, then allow the city economy or production buildings to update before checking the totals.

### World & Environment

Build and expand the stronghold with roads, farms, residences, workshops, warehouses, barracks, and walls. Trainer options for construction, production, population, farming, and logistics are most useful between major actions. Disable accelerated options before entering diplomacy screens, changing seasons, or saving if a feature affects an active timer.

### Combat & Weapons

Recruit troops, organize the army, and defend the city against invasions and sieges. Combat assistance can improve army endurance and reduce losses during real-time battles involving formations, fortress defenses, and large numbers of soldiers. Enable combat options before deployment and disable them after the engagement to preserve normal campaign progression.
